    How it counts

    1. Fly into the field: the hub must see you airborne (60 kt or more) within 25 nm of it.
    2. Stop on it: 35 kt or less, within 1.5 nm of the field. Fields close to another airport count only within half the distance to it.
    3. The first aircraft to do it gets the field. Spawning there, touch-and-goes, and slewing in don't count.

    Where the list comes from

    StatSim's flight logs for every flight to or from the US since January 2020, checked against each field's own StatSim page and refreshed daily. Fields are from OurAirports: open airports and seaplane bases in the US and its territories, heliports left out. List as of —.

    Caveats

    Check your sim has the field before you go; small private strips are often missing. A landing while the hub is offline isn't recorded; ask a VATFLOW admin to add it. StatSim only logs flights with a flight plan, so a field on this list may have had an unlogged visit.

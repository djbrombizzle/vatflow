    ARRIVALS

    Gate crossing = the flight's closest pass within 8 nm of its gate fix (the STAR's namesake fix, from the filed route). In-trail = the time between two crossings no more than 8 minutes apart times the follower's groundspeed, against the MIT the Airport TMU program held that gate to at the time. Airborne delay = time from 150 nm to touchdown, less the 10th percentile for the same gate. Holding = a turn through 330° or more within 9 minutes, 25–200 nm out (same rule as VATSMART).

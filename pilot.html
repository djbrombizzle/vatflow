Pilot ACARS help

GETTING STARTED

  1. Sign in with VATSIM and enter your SimBrief username (or pilot ID). Save.
  2. Plan the flight in SimBrief with Runway Analysis turned on, for the same city pair you file on VATSIM.
  3. Connect to VATSIM under a DAL callsign (e.g. DAL1700) with a departure time in your flight plan.
  4. Log your aircraft's ACARS into Hoppie with your Hoppie logon code, using the same callsign.
  5. Watch "Your flight": it shows Hoppie status, your departure time and when each message is due.

WHAT YOU GET, AND WHEN

EFSR5 min after you are seen on both VATSIM and Hoppie
Planned push updateOnly if you connect 30 to 15 min before departure: 10 min after you connect, moving departure 5 min earlier
Pre-pushback8 min before departure
WDRAt a random time in the last 5 min before departure

Departure time is the one in your VATSIM flight plan (P-time). Messages arrive in that order, at least a minute apart. Connecting late is fine: anything overdue is sent in order, but the pre-pushback and WDR are skipped more than 30 min after P-time. Nothing is sent once you start your takeoff roll.

RUNWAY CHANGE (T/O PERF)

If the airport turns around, send a free-text telex to DALOPS, for example T/O PERF 16R (TO PERF RWY 16R or RWY CHANGE 16R also work). Within about 20 seconds you get a revised WDR for that runway, and the pre-pushback and WDR use it from then on. The numbers come from your SimBrief runway analysis; if the runway is not in it, the reply lists the runways that are.

READING THE WDR

  • D40/05: Dry or Wet runway (wet when the live METAR shows precipitation), flex temp 40 (or TO for full thrust), flaps 5.
  • RATOW: max takeoff weight for that runway. V1, VR, V2 are SimBrief's.
  • The header wind is the live METAR's head/crosswind for that runway.

NEW PLAN?

Every message is built from your latest SimBrief plan when it is sent. After regenerating, press Re-import SimBrief OFP to update the preview here.

NOT GETTING MESSAGES?

  • "Your flight" says why it is waiting: not connected, callsign not DAL, Hoppie not seen, or a SimBrief plan for a different city pair (or more than 18 hours old).
  • Check your ACARS client is logged on to Hoppie as the same callsign you are flying.
  • Make sure "Send me automatic ACARS messages" is ticked and saved.

Fuel by tank, fuel already on board, CG, stab trim and the fueler are modelled from your plan; weights, fuel and takeoff performance are SimBrief's.

Fly a DAL callsign with your ACARS client connected to Hoppie and VATFLOW sends your flight messages from DALOPS, built from your latest SimBrief plan: the EFSR (fuel slip) 5 minutes after you are seen on VATSIM and Hoppie; if you connect 30 to 15 minutes before your filed departure time, a planned push update 10 minutes after you connect moving your departure 5 minutes earlier; the pre-pushback message 8 minutes before your departure time, and the WDR (final weights and takeoff data) at a random time in the 5 minutes before your departure time. Turn on Runway Analysis in SimBrief to get takeoff numbers in the WDR. Runway change? Send a telex to DALOPS such as T/O PERF 16R and you get a revised WDR for that runway; the pre-pushback and WDR use it from then on.

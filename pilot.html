PILOT ACARS Ops Center · automatic company messages over Hoppie
Fly a DAL callsign with your ACARS client connected to Hoppie and VATFLOW sends your flight three messages from DALOPS, built from your latest SimBrief plan: the EFSR (fuel slip) 5 minutes after you are seen on VATSIM and Hoppie, the pre-pushback message 8 minutes before your filed departure time, and the WDR (final weights and takeoff data) at a random time in the 5 minutes before your filed departure time. Turn on Runway Analysis in SimBrief to get takeoff numbers in the WDR.

Sign in with your VATSIM account to set up your pilot profile. VATFLOW only uses your CID to find your flight on the network.

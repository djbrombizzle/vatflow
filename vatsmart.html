VATSMART pulls together live VATSIM traffic, the field's Airport TMU program, EDCTs and ground stops, the NWS forecast, SIGMETs and the VATSIM event calendar, looks three hours ahead with the same rolling-demand and MIT-per-gate math as the rate calculator, and tells you what to do next.

It never changes a program. Set rates on Airport TMU and issue ground delays from an FCA (FCA Builder / IDST).

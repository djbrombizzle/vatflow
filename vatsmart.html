Pick the event field above. VATSMART pulls together live VATSIM traffic, the field's Airport TMU program, EDCTs and ground stops, the METAR, TAF and NWS forecast, departure taxi-out times and the VATSIM event calendar, looks three hours ahead with the same rolling-demand and MIT-per-gate math as the rate calculator, and tells you what to do next.

It never changes a program. Set rates on Airport TMU and issue ground delays from an FCA (FCA Builder / IDST).

    Inspired by the FAA's SMART (Strategic Management of Airspace, Routes and Trajectories), which brings schedules, weather and capacity into one view about two hours ahead. Here the numbers come from the same code as Airport TMU (rate calculator, MIT per gate, Event planner) and the FCA Builder's MIT Monitor. Demand counts arrivals with a filed route to the field; ETAs over each gate fix come from the FCA Builder's engine (filed route through the STAR, climb profile, filed TAS corrected for winds aloft; ground flights leave in about 3 minutes or at a filed departure time within 6 hours), and landing is the rest of the route at 260 kt. Weather from api.weather.gov (US only).

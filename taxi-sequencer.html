Intervals (FAA JO 7110.65)

Times are takeoff roll to takeoff roll. Both aircraft accelerate the same way, so the gap when the follower lifts off is about the time between rolls × the climb groundspeed. Off the same runway, runway separation comes first and the 1 NM is already there by lift-off. The 1 NM rule does not apply when wake turbulence separation is required. Parallels under 2,500 ft apart take the miles and wake rules but not runway separation; runways 2,500 ft or more apart release together when courses split 15°+ (5-8-3a2) and only need in-trail spacing when they don't, with no wake rule.

Wheels-up estimates are present position to the runway at 13 kt along a 1.35× path, plus 90 s to push and start for aircraft that have not moved yet.

Read-only advice. The departure fix is the SID's enroute transition when one is filed, else the next fix in the route, else the SID's exit fix (FAA CIFP). Runways come from the field's VATSIM ATIS or from the chips above. Each runway's queue is ordered by searching every order within the jump limit of first-come first-served for the least total wait.

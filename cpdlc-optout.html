KUSA CPDLC NOTICES Opt out of the "CPDLC is now available" telex

What this is

When a center controller opens KUSA CPDLC on vUSAlink, aircraft flying in that center's airspace that are connected to VATSIM and to Hoppie ACARS get one free-text telex from KUSA:

CPDLC IS NOW AVAILABLE LOGON TO KUSA IF YOU WISH TO OPT OUT PLEASE VISIT VATFLOW.IO

Aircraft already logged on to KUSA don't get it, and an aircraft gets it at most once an hour. To use CPDLC, log on to KUSA from your aircraft's ATC / CPDLC page.

Opt out

Sign in with your VATSIM account. The notice is matched to the CID you fly under, so opting out covers every callsign and aircraft you connect with.
